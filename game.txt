Living Room
The small living room contains a couch, coffee table, and two
 armchairs. Pictures of various cousins adorn the walls along
 with two of your aunt's wretched attempts at oil painting. You
 can't help but notice that the carpet could use a good
 vacuuming.

Doorways lead west to your father's office, north to your
 bedroom, and east to the kitchen.
#
Bedroom
This is your domain and has been since your family moved here
 when you were six. A nightstand with a Superman lamp sits next
 to your twin bed. Posters of various anime characters cover one
 wall. A pile of laundry, slowly but surely attaining sentience,
 occupies the far corner.

 The door to the attic is in the ceiling above the pile of
 clothes. 
 
 You can return south to the living room or go up to the attic.
#
Kitchen
 This is the kitchen. There is a fridge sitting in the corner of the room. You could open it and get something to eat, but you are not that hungry. There is a knife you can take for self-defense or a flashlight to see. There's also a weird orb. Don't touch it.

Doorways lead west to the living room and south to the garage.
#
Office
This is your father's office. There is a small desk with some compartments. In one of the compartments is the key to the car! Freedom has never been so close!

Doorways lead east to the living room
#
Attic
This is the attic. There are some boxes hiding towards the back wall. It is really, really dusty in here. Besides, the boxes probably only contain like, yearbooks or something else that's boring.

 There is a door back down to your bedroom.
#
Garage
This is the garage. The car is in the middle of the room, but it is not currently started. There's miscellaneous, useless junk scattering the floor and tables.

There is a doorway leading north to the kitchen/
